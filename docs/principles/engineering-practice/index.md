# Engineering Practice

## Overview

Engineering practices determine how readily systems can be understood, changed, and operated. These principles keep engineering work repeatable and traceable while preserving human accountability.

Choose a principle below to read its full reasoning.

## Principles

### [Simplicity and Maintainability](simplicity-maintainability.md)

Systems remain understandable and changeable by limiting unnecessary complexity and actively maintaining their design and code.

### [Reuse](reuse.md)

Evaluate existing capabilities before building a custom solution, and make reusable capabilities straightforward to adopt.

### [Everything as Code](everything-as-code.md)

System definitions are kept in version control as the authoritative source for reviewing changes and recreating the intended state.

### [Version Control](version-control.md)

Keep repository branches bounded, traceable, and independent of deployment environments.

### [Automation](automation.md)

Automate repeatable work through maintained code while preserving human judgement and review for decisions that require them.

### [Fast Feedback](fast-feedback.md)

Feedback is clear, reliable, and available at the earliest effective stage.

### [AI-Assisted Engineering](ai-assisted-engineering.md)

AI-assisted work meets the same engineering standards as other work, with scrutiny and controls reflecting its impact and risks.
