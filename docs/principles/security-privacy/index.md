# Security and Privacy

## Overview

Design and engineering decisions limit access, exposure, and risk throughout a system's lifecycle. These principles guide how systems stay secure and protect the personal or health data they handle.

Choose a principle below to read its full reasoning.

## Principles

### [Security Engineering](security-engineering.md)

Security controls limit exposure and remain effective throughout a system's lifecycle.

### [Data Privacy](data-privacy.md)

Limiting exposure of personal or health data throughout its lifecycle protects privacy.
