# Reliability and Operations

## Overview

Reliable services keep working as demand changes and failures occur, and engineers can understand their behaviour. These principles make clear how services handle load and failure, so engineers can verify that behaviour before production reveals gaps.

Choose a principle below to read its full reasoning.

## Principles

### [Reliability and Resilience](reliability-resilience.md)

Services limit the effects of failure and overload, keep safely supported functionality available, and have tested recovery paths.

### [Performance and Scalability](performance-scalability.md)

Service designs explicitly address performance and scaling, with decisions tested against realistic demand.

### [Observability](observability.md)

Services produce consistent telemetry that engineers can link across services to explain behaviour, make operational decisions, and respond to incidents.
