# Data

## Overview

Systems keep data trustworthy by protecting its quality, integrity, and traceability and meeting its jurisdiction requirements throughout its lifecycle. These principles apply when data enters a system, changes, moves, or is recovered.

Choose a principle below to read its full reasoning.

## Principles

### [Data Residency and Sovereignty](data-residency-sovereignty.md)

Data's jurisdiction requirements apply throughout its lifecycle.

### [Data Quality and Integrity](data-quality-integrity.md)

Data remains valid, consistent, and traceable throughout its lifecycle.
