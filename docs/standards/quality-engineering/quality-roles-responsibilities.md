# Quality Roles & Responsibilities

## Defined Quality Roles and Responsibilities

### Summary

Quality and testing activities have documented role assignments that establish responsibility and accountability across the delivery lifecycle.

### Standards

1. Delivery teams and vendors working on the same product or service **MUST** maintain a shared, documented responsibility model covering every quality and testing activity within their delivery scope across planning, design, implementation, verification, release, and operation. `std-qe-defined-quality-responsibilities-01`
2. For each quality and testing activity, the responsibility model **MUST** identify the role accountable for its outcome and each role responsible for performing it. `std-qe-defined-quality-responsibilities-02`
3. Delivery teams and vendors **MUST** update the responsibility model when a change to scope, delivery parties, or role assignments makes it inaccurate. `std-qe-defined-quality-responsibilities-03`

## Assigned Activities and Shared Quality Ownership

### Summary

Quality responsibilities are allocated across the roles whose decisions and work affect product or service quality.

### Standards

1. The responsibility model **MUST** assign quality responsibilities to each product, business, engineering, delivery, operations, support, supplier, and QA role whose decisions or work affect product or service quality. `std-qe-shared-quality-ownership-01`
2. A role accountable for work **MUST** retain accountability for the quality outcomes within that work when another role supports, reviews, tests, or assures it. `std-qe-shared-quality-ownership-02`
3. QA **MUST NOT** be assigned sole accountability for product or service quality or for a quality outcome controlled by another role. `std-qe-shared-quality-ownership-03`

## QA Oversight Across Quality Activities

### Summary

The QA function has visibility of and evaluates quality and testing activities regardless of which role performs them.

### Standards

1. The QA function **MUST** have access to the responsibility model and the scope, status, evidence, findings, gaps, and accepted risks for every quality and testing activity. `std-qe-qa-oversight-01`
2. The QA function **MUST** review planned and completed quality and testing activities to identify omissions, overlaps, inconsistent approaches, and evidence that is insufficient to support the stated outcome or decision. `std-qe-qa-oversight-02`
3. The QA function **MUST** record and communicate each material concern identified through its oversight to the responsible and accountable roles and to any decision-maker relying on the affected evidence. `std-qe-qa-oversight-03`
4. QA oversight **MUST NOT** transfer responsibility for performing an activity or accountability for its outcome or related decisions unless the role assignments are explicitly changed in the responsibility model. `std-qe-qa-oversight-04`
