# Evidence & Reporting

## Accessible Quality Evidence

### Summary

Quality evidence is retained in a shared location that remains accessible to the responsible team and decision-makers without depending on the person who created it.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-accessible-quality-evidence-01` A team or vendor that produces quality evidence **MUST** store it in a designated shared location accessible to the team responsible for the product or service and to the decision-makers who rely on it.
2. `std-qe-accessible-quality-evidence-02` Quality evidence **MUST NOT** be stored solely in personal files, private messages, an individual's account, or a tool that its intended reviewers cannot access.
3. `std-qe-accessible-quality-evidence-03` Quality evidence **MUST** remain accessible for as long as it supports an unresolved finding, accepted risk, or current quality decision.

## Complete Quality Reporting

### Summary

Quality reporting presents the tested scope, outcomes, material gaps, open findings, and accepted risks.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-complete-quality-reporting-01` A quality report **MUST** identify the scope assessed and the outcomes of that assessment.
2. `std-qe-complete-quality-reporting-02` A quality report **MUST** identify each known material gap, open finding, and accepted risk.
3. `std-qe-complete-quality-reporting-03` A pass rate, coverage measure, or other aggregate quality measure **MUST NOT** be presented without the scope and known limitations needed to interpret it.

## Actionable Findings

### Summary

Quality findings contain the information needed to understand the problem, assess its impact, and begin the required or recommended follow-up.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-actionable-findings-01` A quality finding **MUST** describe the observed outcome, the expected outcome, the affected area, and the conditions under which the problem occurred.
2. `std-qe-actionable-findings-02` A quality finding **MUST** state its impact and include or reference the supporting evidence and diagnostic information needed to investigate it.
3. `std-qe-actionable-findings-03` A quality finding **MUST** state the required or recommended follow-up, such as further investigation, correction, a risk decision, or retesting if necessary.

## Shared Defect Standard

### Summary

Delivery teams and vendors use a shared standard to record, classify, prioritise, manage, verify, and close defects consistently.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-shared-defect-standard-01` Delivery teams and vendors working on the same product or service **MUST** define and follow a shared defect standard that specifies required record content, classification criteria, lifecycle statuses, ownership, dispositions, verification, and closure criteria.

## Current and Traceable Evidence

### Summary

Quality evidence identifies its source and tested context so its applicability to the current product version and conditions can be established.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-current-traceable-evidence-01` Each quality evidence record **MUST** identify the test run or assessment that produced it, when it was produced, the tested environment, the product or service version, and any applicable change, build, or artifact.
2. `std-qe-current-traceable-evidence-02` Quality evidence **MUST NOT** be represented as current when the tested version or conditions have materially changed unless its continuing applicability has been assessed and recorded.
