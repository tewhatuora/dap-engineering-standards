# Outcome Driven Assurance

## Clear Test Outcomes

### Summary

Each testing activity has a defined purpose and an established test outcome.

### Standards

1. A team or vendor responsible for a testing activity **MUST** define, before the activity begins, its purpose and the behaviour, requirement, or risk it is intended to assess. `std-qe-clear-test-outcomes-01`
2. The purpose of a recurring testing activity **SHOULD** be reviewed when the related behaviour, requirement, or risk changes, and the activity **SHOULD** be updated or retired when it no longer provides useful information. `std-qe-clear-test-outcomes-02`
3. Test evidence retained for a testing activity **MUST** have a clear correlation to the test outcome stated for that activity. `std-qe-clear-test-outcomes-04`

## Clear and Useful Test Evidence

### Summary

Test evidence provides accessible information that supports evaluation of the stated outcome and any subsequent quality decision.

### Standards

1. Test evidence **MUST** identify what was tested, the relevant results and findings, and any limitation or gap that affects confidence in the stated outcome. `std-qe-clear-useful-test-evidence-01`
2. Test evidence and reporting data **MUST** be accessible to the team members responsible for evaluating the stated outcome and making subsequent quality decisions. `std-qe-clear-useful-test-evidence-02`
3. Test evidence **MUST** contain enough context for those team members to evaluate the stated outcome without repeating the testing or depending on the person who performed it to interpret the evidence. `std-qe-clear-useful-test-evidence-03`
4. A gap in test evidence **MUST** be raised with Quality Assurance and resolved within an agreed timeframe proportionate to its effect on confidence in the stated outcome. `std-qe-clear-useful-test-evidence-04`

## Quality Acceptance Criteria

### Summary

Acceptance criteria are defined and challenged before implementation so they describe observable, testable conditions that provide an objective and stable basis for evaluating whether work is acceptable.

### Standards

1. Acceptance criteria **MUST** define the relevant starting conditions, event or action, and observable expected result for each product or service behaviour within the scope of the work. `std-qe-quality-acceptance-criteria-01`
2. Quality Assurance **SHOULD** review and challenge acceptance criteria before implementation begins to identify ambiguity, conflicting expectations, and outcomes that cannot be objectively evaluated. `std-qe-quality-acceptance-criteria-02`
3. Each acceptance criterion **MUST** provide enough information to derive one or more tests that objectively determine whether its expected result occurred. `std-qe-quality-acceptance-criteria-03`

## Traceable Assurance Outcomes

### Summary

Expected outcomes and acceptance criteria remain traceable through their tests, results, findings, resolution, and the release decision they support.

### Standards

1. Each expected outcome and acceptance criterion for a feature **MUST** be traceable to the tests intended to verify it. `std-qe-traceable-assurance-outcomes-01`
2. Each test result, evidence record, and finding **MUST** be traceable to its originating test and the expected outcome or acceptance criterion that test verifies. `std-qe-traceable-assurance-outcomes-02`
3. Each test finding **MUST** remain traceable to its current disposition and, when resolved, the evidence that verifies its resolution. `std-qe-traceable-assurance-outcomes-03`
4. A release decision **SHOULD** identify the test evidence on which it relies and any unresolved evidence gap or finding that affects confidence in the decision. `std-qe-traceable-assurance-outcomes-04`
