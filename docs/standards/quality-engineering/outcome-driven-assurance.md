# Outcome Driven Assurance

## Clear Test Outcomes

### Summary

Each testing activity has a defined purpose and an established test outcome.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-clear-test-outcomes-01` A team or vendor responsible for a testing activity **MUST** define, before the activity begins, its purpose and the behaviour, requirement, or risk it is intended to assess.
2. `std-qe-clear-test-outcomes-02` The purpose of a recurring testing activity **SHOULD** be reviewed when the related behaviour, requirement, or risk changes, and the activity **SHOULD** be updated or retired when it no longer provides useful information.
3. `std-qe-clear-test-outcomes-04` Test evidence retained for a testing activity **MUST** have a clear correlation to the test outcome stated for that activity.

## Clear and Useful Test Evidence

### Summary

Test evidence provides accessible information that supports evaluation of the stated outcome and any subsequent quality decision.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-clear-useful-test-evidence-01` Test evidence **MUST** identify what was tested, the relevant results and findings, and any limitation or gap that affects confidence in the stated outcome.
2. `std-qe-clear-useful-test-evidence-02` Test evidence and reporting data **MUST** be accessible to the team members responsible for evaluating the stated outcome and making subsequent quality decisions.
3. `std-qe-clear-useful-test-evidence-03` Test evidence **MUST** contain enough context for those team members to evaluate the stated outcome without repeating the testing or depending on the person who performed it to interpret the evidence.
4. `std-qe-clear-useful-test-evidence-04` A gap in test evidence **MUST** be raised with Quality Assurance and resolved within an agreed timeframe proportionate to its effect on confidence in the stated outcome.

## Quality Acceptance Criteria

### Summary

Acceptance criteria are defined and challenged before implementation so they describe observable, testable conditions that provide an objective and stable basis for evaluating whether work is acceptable.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-quality-acceptance-criteria-01` Acceptance criteria **MUST** define the relevant starting conditions, event or action, and observable expected result for each product or service behaviour within the scope of the work.
2. `std-qe-quality-acceptance-criteria-02` Quality Assurance **SHOULD** review and challenge acceptance criteria before implementation begins to identify ambiguity, conflicting expectations, and outcomes that cannot be objectively evaluated.
3. `std-qe-quality-acceptance-criteria-03` Each acceptance criterion **MUST** provide enough information to derive one or more tests that objectively determine whether its expected result occurred.

## Traceable Assurance Outcomes

### Summary

Expected outcomes and acceptance criteria remain traceable through their tests, results, findings, resolution, and the release decision they support.

### Standards

1. `std-qe-traceable-assurance-outcomes-01` Each expected outcome and acceptance criterion for a feature **MUST** be traceable to the tests intended to verify it.
2. `std-qe-traceable-assurance-outcomes-02` Each test result, evidence record, and finding **MUST** be traceable to its originating test and the expected outcome or acceptance criterion that test verifies.
3. `std-qe-traceable-assurance-outcomes-03` Each test finding **MUST** remain traceable to its current disposition and, when resolved, the evidence that verifies its resolution.
4. `std-qe-traceable-assurance-outcomes-04` A release decision **SHOULD** identify the test evidence on which it relies and any unresolved evidence gap or finding that affects confidence in the decision.
