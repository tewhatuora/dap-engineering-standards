# Cost & Environmental Impact Monitoring & Attribution

Make each service's recurring cost and environmental impact visible and attributable to the team responsible for it.

## Service Cost Attribution

### Summary

A service's recurring cost is measurable separately from aggregate spend and attributable to its responsible team.

### Standards

1. A service's recurring infrastructure, platform, and managed-service cost **MUST** be measurable separately from aggregate organisational spend.
2. A service's measured cost **MUST** be attributed to the team responsible for its design and operation.
3. A service's measured cost **MUST** be recorded over time so a sustained change in its spend can be identified.

### Implements These Principles

- [Cost Awareness](../../principles/cost-sustainability/cost-awareness.md)

## Environmental Impact Attribution

### Summary

A service's energy consumption or estimated carbon footprint is measurable separately from aggregate infrastructure consumption and attributable to its responsible team.

### Standards

1. A service's energy consumption or estimated carbon footprint **MUST** be measurable separately from aggregate infrastructure consumption.
2. A service's measured energy consumption or estimated carbon footprint **MUST** be attributed to the team responsible for its design and operation.
3. A service's environmental impact measurements **SHOULD** be integrated into its existing monitoring and reporting.
