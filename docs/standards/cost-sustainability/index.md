# Cost & Sustainability

## Overview

Cost and sustainability standards require attributable, monitored service costs, measured resource use, and environmental impact quantified where practicable.

Choose a standard below to read it in full.

## Standards

### [Cost & Impact Visibility](cost-impact-visibility.md)

Each service's recurring cost is visible and attributable, its responsible team is alerted to unexpected spend, and its environmental impact is measured.

### [Resource Efficiency & Optimisation](resource-efficiency-optimisation.md)

Compute and storage resources, telemetry, and stored data are managed to meet service requirements without unnecessary cost or resource consumption.
